# How to record so your video looks its best

Share this with users before they record. Show the short version after an edit when the background could not
be swapped (analysis.json `background.keyable` is false).

## Short version (show this)

To get an animated background behind you, record in front of a **green or blue cloth or wall**, stand about
**1 metre in front of it**, and light your face evenly. Avoid cream, beige or wood backgrounds; they're too
close to skin colour.

## Background

| Background | Can it be swapped? |
|---|---|
| Green screen cloth or green wall | Yes, best |
| Blue wall or blue cloth | Yes |
| Dark grey or black wall (don't wear black) | Yes |
| White wall (don't wear white) | Usually |
| Cream, beige, peach, light yellow, wood | No, too close to skin |
| Patterns, shelves, doors, room corners | No |

- Keep the whole frame edge plain: no door, switch, shelf or corner at the sides or top.
- Smooth and wrinkle-free. A green cloth costs about Rs 1,000-2,000; clip it flat.
- Wear a colour different from the background (no green clothes in front of green).

## Light

- Face a window or a lamp so your face is evenly lit.
- Light the background evenly too: no bright patch on one side, no dark corner.
- Stand about 1 metre (3 feet) in front of the background so your shadow doesn't fall on it.

## Camera

- Phone upright (vertical) for reels, at eye level, on a stand or propped up so it doesn't move.
- Record in the phone's normal camera app at 1080p or 4K.
- Send the original file by USB cable or Google Drive. Don't send it through WhatsApp: it shrinks the
  video to about 576x1024 and smears the edges.

## Sound

- A quiet room. Turn off fans, AC and TV if you can.
- A clip-on mic makes the biggest difference; the phone's own mic is fine at arm's length.

## Speaking

- Pause for a second between sentences.
- If you get a line wrong, just say it again. The editor keeps your last take and cuts the earlier one.
