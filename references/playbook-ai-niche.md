## REELS / SHORTS (9:16)

### Layout — the "split stack" (3 of 4 winners use it)
- **Bottom ~55%:** you, talking head, mic in frame, dark moody background with one practical light.
- **Top ~45%:** whatever you're talking about — tool UI, prompt box, screenshot of a tweet/result,
  simple icon diagram (`Your Skill + AI → Sell It Online`).
- When there's nothing to show, go **full-frame face** for 2–4 s (feels like a punch-in).
- Clean, minimal top graphics on a dark background beat busy stock footage.

### Hook (0–2 s)
- **Title card on frame 1**: 2–3 lines, serif or bold type, money number in an accent colour box
  (`If I Had To Make` / **$1,000** / `I Wouldn't Build`). It shows the promise before a word is said.
- Spoken line template: **"If I had to make $X with [tool] in [time], here's exactly what I'd do."**
  Or the contrarian: **"I wouldn't do A, B or C — they're too easy to copy."**
- Add **proof within 6 s**: "this is the same method I taught Tom, who signed an $8,000 client."
- Optional pattern-interrupt: show 3 rejected ideas as thumbnails, then cross them out.

### Captions
- **1–3 words at a time**, ALL CAPS, sitting on the seam between top graphic and face
  (or lower-middle when full-face).
- Style A (Patrick Dang): small white text in a **rounded orange pill** — accent `#E8603C`-ish.
- Style B (Nate Herk): **large italic serif**, white, no box; key word swaps to a colour.
- Style C (AI Master): **bold yellow, black outline**, one word at a time.
- Pick ONE style and keep it on every reel (it becomes your brand). Emphasise money/tool words.

**✅ HOUSE STYLE (decided 26 Nov 2026): Style A — orange pill.**
- White, bold sans, ALL CAPS, 1–3 words, inside a rounded pill `#E8603C` (~85% opacity).
- Sits on the seam between top graphic and face; lower-middle when full-face.
- Money numbers and tool names (e.g. **$1,000**, **CLAUDE**) get a bigger pill or a scale pop.
- Same orange is the accent for the frame-1 title card money box, so captions + title card match.
- Why: it's the style on the two strongest Shorts in the sample (90x, 36x), it stays readable on
  both dark sets and bright screen recordings, and it doesn't cover your face like big yellow text.

### Pacing
- Zero pauses: cut every breath. Delivery is fast (~200+ words/min after the cut).
- A visual change (graphic swap, face↔full-frame, zoom) **every 2–4 s**.
- Length: **50–85 s** is where these winners sit.

### Ending
- End on a line, not a trail-off: "Comment **CLAUDE** and I'll send you the prompt."
- Or loop: the last line sets up the first title card.

## LONG-FORM (16:9, 10–35 min)

### Cold open (0–30 s) — no logo, no "hey guys"
1. **Problem in sentence one** ("you're busy with a full-time job and don't have time…").
2. **Promise**: what this video gives them (the list / the method).
3. **Credibility/proof** by second 15–25: a client name + result, revenue number, screenshot.
   Show it as **B-roll with a name lower-third** (photo + "Shawn — insurance sales manager, no AI experience").
4. **Open loop**: "the third one is the one that's made me the most money personally."

### Structure
- **List format wins** in this niche: numbered items, each a mini-section.
- **Chapter cards** between items: a short animated or stylised card with `chapter 1 / FOUNDATIONS`
  (Liam Ottley) or a bold word card (`BROKE`, `AI TikTok explainer videos` — Its Annie).
- Inside each item: **talk to camera → screen recording of the actual tool → result/proof → back to camera.**

### Pacing (measured, first 3 min)
| Channel style | Visual change every | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Fast list (Its Annie, Liam Ottley) | **2–3 s** | beginner side-hustle lists |
| Mid (Patrick Dang) | **~5–6 s** | talking-head + proof |
| Slow demo (Nate Herk) | **~15 s**, face cam stays in a corner bubble | live screen demos/experiments |

Rule of thumb: never sit on the same unmoving face shot for more than ~8 s — jump-cut zoom, B-roll,
or a screenshot.

### Screen recordings
- Face cam as a **small rounded box bottom-left** over the screen recording (Nate Herk), so you
  never fully leave the frame.
- **Zoom into** the part of the UI you're talking about; highlight the line you're reading.
- Show **real results**: Discord/WhatsApp messages from clients, dashboards, payment numbers,
  big animated number reveal (`$104,219`).

### Look
- Dark, moody set; one warm practical light; mic in shot. Slight contrast + warmth grade.
- Text: one bold sans + one italic/serif accent; one accent colour (orange or yellow) used everywhere.
- Music: low, constant lo-fi/ambient bed under the voice; tiny whoosh on chapter cards.

## B-roll: what to capture (you record these, I cut them in)

The winners use almost **no stock footage**. Their B-roll is proof and the real tools:

1. **Screen recordings of the AI tool doing the thing.** Record at 1920×1080, Windows key + Alt + R
   or OBS. Make one clip per step you talk about. Type slowly, and pause 1 s after each result appears.
2. **Results/proof screenshots**: client WhatsApp/DM messages, payment received, dashboards,
   view counts, followers. Blur names unless you have permission.
3. **"Bad options" thumbnails** for cross-out hooks: screenshots of other people's video titles or
   app icons. Use them small and briefly, as commentary.
4. **One or two lifestyle shots** (optional): laptop at night, typing close-up, city view. Phone is fine.
   If missing, I can pull free stock from Pexels (free licence) per video.
5. **A clean photo of each person you mention** (with their OK) for the name lower-third.

Recording yourself: phone or camera **vertical 1080×1920** for reels, horizontal 4K/1080 for long
form, and position the camera at eye level. Use a dark background, one warm lamp behind you and a mic in shot.
Leave a 1 s pause between takes. I cut the pauses, and they make retakes easy to find.

## Your edit checklist (what I'll apply to your footage)

**Reel**
- [ ] Remove dead air + retakes (`yt-edit` / `deadair.py`)
- [ ] Re-write first line as a money+tool+time hook (`yt-script` hookscore)
- [ ] Frame-1 title card with accent money box
- [ ] 9:16 split stack: graphic top, face bottom; full-face punch-ins every few lines
- [ ] 1–3 word caps captions in one house style
- [ ] Visual change every 2–4 s; 50–85 s total
- [ ] End on a comment-keyword CTA or loop line

**Long-form**
- [ ] Remove dead air + retakes
- [ ] Cold open: problem → promise → proof (with B-roll) → open loop, under 30 s
- [ ] Numbered sections with chapter cards
- [ ] Screen recordings with face bubble + zoom-ins on the relevant UI
- [ ] Visual change at least every ~8 s
- [ ] Chapters for the description (`yt-chapters`), title+thumbnail pair (`yt-package`), description (`yt-seo`)
- [ ] 3–5 Shorts cut from it (`yt-shorts`)
